01Where is…?地铁站在哪里？ · dì tiě zhàn zài nǎ lǐ?›

地铁站在哪里？

dì tiě zhàn zài nǎ lǐ?

Where is the subway station?

在哪里 asks where something is.

02Left & right前面左转。 · qián miàn zuǒ zhuǎn.›

前面左转。

qián miàn zuǒ zhuǎn.

Turn left ahead.

左转 = turn left; 右转 = turn right.

03Straight ahead一直往前走。 · yì zhí wǎng qián zǒu.›

一直往前走。

yì zhí wǎng qián zǒu.

Go straight ahead.

一直 means continuously/straight on.

04Taking the subway我要坐地铁去机场。 · wǒ yào zuò dì tiě qù jī chǎng.›

我要坐地铁去机场。

wǒ yào zuò dì tiě qù jī chǎng.

I want to take the subway to the airport.

坐 is used for taking transport.

05Buying a ticket一张去上海的票。 · yì zhāng qù shàng hǎi de piào.›

一张去上海的票。

yì zhāng qù shàng hǎi de piào.

One ticket to Shanghai.

张 is the measure word for tickets.

06Travel time到那里要多久？ · dào nà lǐ yào duō jiǔ?›

到那里要多久？

dào nà lǐ yào duō jiǔ?

How long does it take to get there?

多久 asks “how long”.

07Hotel check-in我有预订。 · wǒ yǒu yù dìng.›

我有预订。

wǒ yǒu yù dìng.

I have a reservation.

预订 means to reserve/book.

08Taxi phrases请送我到这个地址。 · qǐng sòng wǒ dào zhè ge dì zhǐ.›

请送我到这个地址。

qǐng sòng wǒ dào zhè ge dì zhǐ.

Please take me to this address.

送…到… means take/deliver someone to a place.

09Getting help我迷路了。 · wǒ mí lù le.›

我迷路了。

wǒ mí lù le.

I am lost.

迷路 means to get lost.
