OFFLINE HSK 1 REFERENCE

HSK 1 · Beginner foundations

Very simple everyday Chinese: greetings, identity, numbers, time and basic needs.

Key topics

  • Greetings and introductions
  • Numbers, dates and time
  • Family and people
  • Food and common objects
  • Basic question words: 什么、谁、哪儿、几

Grammar to revise

  • 是 for identity
  • 有 for possession/existence
  • 吗 and 呢 questions
  • 不 / 没 for negation
  • 的 for possession

Revision strategy

Focus on recognizing high-frequency words quickly and matching short spoken phrases to meaning.

Offline study checklist

  • Review vocabulary in short daily sessions
  • Read examples aloud
  • Write 5 original sentences
  • Complete the BambooByte HSK 1 mock test
  • Review mistakes before moving on
