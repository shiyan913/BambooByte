OFFLINE HSK 5 REFERENCE

HSK 5 · Advanced comprehension

Read longer authentic-style texts and express ideas in greater detail.

Key topics

  • News and public life
  • Culture and history
  • Workplace communication
  • Environment and society
  • Abstract opinions

Grammar to revise

  • 之所以…是因为…
  • 与其…不如…
  • 既然…就…
  • 尽管…可是…
  • 一方面…另一方面…

Revision strategy

Build formal vocabulary, collocations and reading stamina. Summarize paragraphs in your own words.

Offline study checklist

  • Review vocabulary in short daily sessions
  • Read examples aloud
  • Write 5 original sentences
  • Complete the BambooByte HSK 5 mock test
  • Review mistakes before moving on
