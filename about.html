OUR PROJECT

Why BambooByte?

We wanted to create an adult-friendly Chinese learning experience inspired by the clarity and motivation of modern language apps while giving it a distinct bamboo-and-panda identity. The site includes themed lessons, HSK practice, mock tests, grammar, reading, idioms and personalised progress.

🎋 Clear progression🐼 Friendly identity🀄 Practical Chinese💻 Interactive learning
