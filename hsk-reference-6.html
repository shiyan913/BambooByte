OFFLINE HSK 6 REFERENCE

HSK 6 · High-level Chinese

Understand complex written/spoken Chinese and express nuanced opinions fluently.

Key topics

  • Formal and academic language
  • Argument and inference
  • Literary-style expressions
  • Idioms and fixed phrases
  • Long-form listening and reading

Grammar to revise

  • Advanced conjunctions
  • Register and nuance
  • Four-character expressions
  • Condensed written structures
  • Logical transitions

Revision strategy

Read for implication, author attitude and structure. Practice concise summaries and precise word choice.

Offline study checklist

  • Review vocabulary in short daily sessions
  • Read examples aloud
  • Write 5 original sentences
  • Complete the BambooByte HSK 6 mock test
  • Review mistakes before moving on
