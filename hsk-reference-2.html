OFFLINE HSK 2 REFERENCE

HSK 2 · Everyday Chinese

Simple direct communication about familiar daily topics.

Key topics

  • Daily routines
  • Shopping and prices
  • Weather and transport
  • Hobbies and preferences
  • Locations and directions

Grammar to revise

  • 正在 for actions in progress
  • 过 for past experience
  • 比 for comparison
  • 因为…所以…
  • 虽然…但是…

Revision strategy

Practice short dialogues and build speed with common two-part questions and answers.

Offline study checklist

  • Review vocabulary in short daily sessions
  • Read examples aloud
  • Write 5 original sentences
  • Complete the BambooByte HSK 2 mock test
  • Review mistakes before moving on
