OFFLINE HSK 4 REFERENCE

HSK 4 · Confident communication

Discuss a wider range of topics and communicate more fluently.

Key topics

  • Education and careers
  • Technology and media
  • Relationships and society
  • Culture and travel
  • Problem solving

Grammar to revise

  • 不但…而且…
  • 只要…就…
  • 只有…才…
  • 即使…也…
  • 无论…都…

Revision strategy

Train paragraph reading, connector words and sentence re-ordering. Pay attention to context and tone.

Offline study checklist

  • Review vocabulary in short daily sessions
  • Read examples aloud
  • Write 5 original sentences
  • Complete the BambooByte HSK 4 mock test
  • Review mistakes before moving on
